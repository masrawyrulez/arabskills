# ArabSkills (شركة مهارات العرب للحلول المتكاملة)

> Egypt’s Premier TVET & Applied Technology Schools (ATS) Development Firm

ArabSkills is an Egypt-based educational development and consulting firm specialized in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET), Applied Technology Schools (ATS), institutional transformation, and international accreditation alignment.

## Key Features
- **Modern Responsive Portal:** Redesigned with executive corporate styling, bilingual support, and verified case studies.
- **Strategic Partners Showcase:** Highlighting projects with UN agencies (WFP, UNHCR, IOM), bilateral donors (GIZ, USAID), and industry leaders (SODIC, Knauf, Viatris, ArabcoMed, Daltex, Sawiris Foundation).
- **Online Certificate Registry:** Instant credential verification portal for ATS and vocational training graduates.
- **Google Sheets Integration:** Live synchronization with shared Google Sheets to manage student certificates effortlessly without database management.
- **1-Click Local Server:** Easy `start-server.bat` launcher for local preview.

## Local Setup
1. Double click `start-server.bat` to launch the local development server.
2. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` in your web browser.
